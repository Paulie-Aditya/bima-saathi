# Suraksha Family Floater – Policy Wording (SAMPLE)

Invented sample wording for the Bima Saathi hackathon demo. It follows the structure of standard Indian
health policy wordings but is not the wording of any real insurer or product.

## 1. Preamble
1.1 Preamble. This policy is a contract between the Insurer and the Policyholder. The Insurer will pay for hospitalisation expenses of the Insured Persons named in the Schedule, subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, sub-limits and the Sum Insured stated in the Schedule.

## 2. Definitions
2.1 Hospitalisation. Admission in a hospital for a minimum period of 24 consecutive in-patient care hours, except for the day care procedures listed in Annexure I, where the 24-hour limit does not apply.
2.2 Network Provider. A hospital enlisted by the Insurer or its TPA to provide medical services to an Insured Person through a cashless facility.
2.3 Cashless Facility. A facility under which payment for treatment is made by the Insurer directly to the Network Provider, to the extent pre-authorisation is approved.
2.4 Co-payment. A cost-sharing requirement under which the Insured Person bears a stated percentage of the admissible claim amount. Co-payment does not reduce the Sum Insured.
2.5 Waiting Period. The period from the first policy start date during which specified diseases or treatments are not covered. Continuous renewal without a break counts towards the waiting period.
2.6 Room Rent. The amount charged by a hospital for occupying a bed per day, including the associated nursing and boarding charges.
2.7 Day Care Treatment. Medical treatment or surgery done under general or local anaesthesia in a hospital or day care centre in less than 24 hours because of technological advancement.

## 3. Coverage
3.1 In-patient Hospitalisation. The Insurer will pay medical expenses for room rent, nursing, ICU, surgeon and doctor fees, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation theatre charges, medicines, drugs and diagnostics incurred during hospitalisation for at least 24 hours.
3.2 Pre-hospitalisation. Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before the date of admission, for the same condition for which hospitalisation was required, are covered.
3.3 Post-hospitalisation. Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge, for the same condition, are covered.
3.4 Day Care Procedures. Day care procedures listed in Annexure I, such as cataract surgery, dialysis and chemotherapy, are covered even if hospitalisation is less than 24 hours.
3.5 Road Ambulance. Expenses for road ambulance to the nearest hospital are covered up to Rs 2,000 per hospitalisation.
3.6 Maternity. Expenses for delivery, including caesarean section, are covered after a waiting period of 9 months from the first policy start date, up to Rs 50,000 for normal delivery and Rs 60,000 for caesarean delivery.

## 4. Limits and cost sharing
4.1 Sum Insured. The Insurer's total liability for all claims in a policy year shall not exceed the Sum Insured shown in the Schedule.
4.2 Room Rent Limit. Room rent is payable up to Rs 5,000 per day for a normal room and Rs 10,000 per day for ICU. If the Insured Person occupies a room with a higher rent than this limit, all associated medical expenses, except medicines, consumables, implants and diagnostics, will be paid in the same proportion as the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent.
4.3 Co-payment. A co-payment of 10% applies to every admissible claim. For Insured Persons aged 60 years or more at the time of hospitalisation under an individual plan, a co-payment of 20% applies.
4.4 Sub-limits. The following sub-limits apply per policy year: cataract surgery Rs 40,000 per eye; total knee replacement Rs 2,00,000 per knee; total hip replacement Rs 2,00,000.
4.5 Non-payable Items. Items listed in Annexure II, such as admission kits, toiletries and gloves not used for a medical procedure, are not payable.

## 5. Waiting periods
5.1 Initial Waiting Period. Expenses for any illness, except an accident, in the first 30 days from the first policy start date are excluded.
5.2 Pre-existing Diseases. Expenses related to a pre-existing disease and its direct complications are excluded until 36 months of continuous coverage have elapsed.
5.3 Specified Diseases and Procedures. The following are covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage: cataract, hernia, hysterectomy, knee and hip replacement unless due to an accident, kidney and gall bladder stones, piles and fissures, sinusitis and tonsillitis, spine surgery and ligament repair.
5.4 Bariatric Surgery. Surgery for obesity is covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage and only if the body mass index is 40 or more.
5.5 Accidents. Waiting periods in 5.1 to 5.3 do not apply to hospitalisation caused by an accident.

## 6. Exclusions
6.1 Cosmetic Treatment. Cosmetic or plastic surgery is excluded unless needed for reconstruction after an accident, burns or cancer.
6.2 Refractive Error. Treatment for correction of eyesight due to refractive error below 7.5 dioptres, such as LASIK, is excluded.
6.3 Infertility. Treatment for infertility and assisted reproduction, including IVF, is excluded.
6.4 Dental Treatment. Dental treatment is excluded unless it needs hospitalisation because of an accident.
6.5 Unproven Treatment. Treatments that are not supported by acceptable clinical evidence are excluded.
6.6 Self-inflicted Injury and Substance Abuse. Treatment arising from intentional self-injury or the abuse of alcohol or drugs is excluded.

## 7. Claims procedure
7.1 Cashless Claims. For planned hospitalisation, the Insured Person or the Network Provider must request pre-authorisation at least 48 hours before admission. For emergencies, the request must be made within 24 hours of admission. The Insurer will decide on a cashless request within 1 hour of receiving complete documents.
7.2 Documents for Pre-authorisation. The pre-authorisation form signed by the hospital, the treating doctor's advice for admission, relevant investigation reports, the policy e-card and a photo ID of the patient.
7.3 Final Cashless Approval. The Insurer will give final authorisation within 3 hours of receiving the discharge request from the hospital.
7.4 Reimbursement Claims. The claim must be submitted within 30 days of discharge with the claim form, original bills and receipts, discharge summary, investigation reports, prescriptions, a cancelled cheque and photo ID.
7.5 Claim Settlement. The Insurer will settle or reject a claim within 30 days of receiving the last necessary document. If the Insurer needs more documents, it will ask for all of them at once.
7.6 Query Response. If the Insured Person does not send requested documents within 15 days, the Insurer will send reminders. The claim may be closed if documents are not received within 30 days of the first query, and can be reopened when they arrive.

## 8. Grievances
8.1 Grievance Redressal. The Insured Person may write to the Insurer's grievance cell or call the toll-free number. The Insurer will respond within 14 days.
8.2 Insurance Ombudsman. If not satisfied, the Insured Person may approach the Insurance Ombudsman within one year of the Insurer's final reply.
8.3 Free Look Period. A new policy may be cancelled within 30 days of receipt if the Insured Person does not agree with its terms, with a refund of premium minus proportionate risk premium and medical check-up costs.
